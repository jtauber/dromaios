A processor specification can be more than a reference. It can be the source of the emulator itself.

Each guide brings together the chip’s history, its programming model, and formal definitions of its behavior. Dromaios turns those definitions into working processor models. The explanation and the machine share one source.

Interactive lessons and embedded emulators will let you experiment with these processors in your browser. Today, you can explore their guides here and build and run the models from the repository.
